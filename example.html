How to play

01
Reach the goal first

Two players race to the opposite edge. With 3–4 players, the first pawn to enter the center square wins.

02
Pentagon mode

Select 5 ◆ for the five-sided board. The host may start with 1–5 players, and the first pawn to reach its center wins.

03
Move or place a wall

Take one action per turn. On the pentagon board, follow a connected line; walls block one connection.

04
Never close every path

Every player must retain at least one route to the goal. Five-player rooms always use Pentagon mode.
