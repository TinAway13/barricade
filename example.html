How to play

01
Reach the opposite side

In 2–4 player rooms, each pawn wins by reaching the opposite edge from its starting side on the 9×9 board.

02
Crown mode

Select 5 ♛ for an 11×11 board. Everyone spawns at a random, unique edge tile and races for the crown in the center.

03
Move, jump, or place a wall

Pawns move one tile and can jump across multiple rivals standing consecutively in one line. Crown players receive 8 walls each.

04
Never close every path

Every player must retain at least one route to the goal. The host may restart an active round at any time.
